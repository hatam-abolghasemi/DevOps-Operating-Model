# Contributing

- Ideas and debates: [Discussions](https://github.com/hatam-abolghasemi/devops-operating-model/discussions).
- Problems: open an issue.
- Fixes: open a pull request.

Keep the language plain, and keep the layers agreeing with each other: a change follows the steps in [changing the model](4-practices/changing-the-model.md). Behavior changes go in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).

## Versioning

Releases follow [releasing](4-practices/releasing.md). For this repository:

- **Major:** a core rule changes, or a file, term or convention is renamed or removed.
- **Minor:** something new and optional.
- **Patch:** wording only.
